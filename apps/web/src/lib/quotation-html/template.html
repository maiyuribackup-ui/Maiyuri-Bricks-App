Why mud interlock

Not an alternative. An upgrade.

Red soil, pressed and cured — never kiln fired. The same material Tamil homes were built from, made to a modern tolerance so the blocks lock together and the wall runs true.

Conventional red brick
Traps heat like a pressure cooker.
  • Higher cement and plaster usage
  • Traps humidity indoors
  • Hotter interiors through the afternoon
  • Kiln fired
Maiyuri mud interlock
Breathes naturally like a clay pot.
  • Less mortar, and no plaster needed
  • Breathable walls
  • Naturally cooler interiors
  • No firing, and recyclable
What that means at home
Thermal comfort

Red soil holds heat slowly and gives it back slowly.

Healthier living

Walls that breathe rather than trap damp.

Strong and load-bearing

Tested to IS 15658; load-bearing to G+2, subject to your engineer.

No kiln, no toxins

No fly ash, no kiln firing, no chemical hardeners.

Comfort at home also depends on roof treatment, ventilation, shading, orientation and workmanship — the wall is one part of it.
